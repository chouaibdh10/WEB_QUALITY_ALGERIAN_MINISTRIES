# Design and measurement revision — 20 October 2026

## Design decisions

The existing visual identity and full desktop header/hero composition remain. Published evaluations now appear before the directory controls, with direct report links and a three-part score track: earned, not earned, and unmeasured. Cards are ordered by measurement date, not score. On small screens they form a keyboard-accessible scroll-snap strip. A public revision-record download makes the verification scope discoverable.

Detailed profile routes and their full evidence data load on demand. The final initial JavaScript bundle is 330.66 KB (105.97 KB gzip), versus 511.74 KB before this change. This is a build-size comparison, not a field speed claim. The landing page uses a small generated summary index, checked against each published report.

Domain-confirmed measured sites no longer appear in the awaiting-verification filter. Communication retains its explicit ownership uncertainty.

Health's mobile homepage lab LCP median was 13.91 seconds. The worst page/device median CLS was 0.408. Shared unnamed controls were manually corroborated. The guarded contact DOM timed out; normal-browser follow-up rendered the form. The timeout remains a capture gap, not an outage finding.

Culture's mobile homepage lab LCP median was 33.30 seconds. Normal-browser checks reproduced a 415px document width in 320/390px viewports. The language link contrast was independently calculated at 2.29:1. Initial local filtering interstitials were excluded and preserved; a standard browser-UA follow-up rendered the pages. Numerous guarded subresources were excluded, so the clean automated accessibility result is not treated as conformity.

Interior was excluded because the observed robots policy denied the declared entry. No failure score was assigned. No forms, emails, calls, or external service applications were submitted.

## Revision evidence

`scripts/audit-published-evidence.mjs` separately recomputes every public median and performance award from raw Lighthouse JSON. Across all six published reports it checked 114 successful runs and 381 retained artifact hashes, without arithmetic or hash mismatches. It also checks capture-time certificate validity/trust and header-absence claims. Manual rationales were reread against retained observations. The public summary is `public/evaluations/results-revision.json`; private DOM, screenshots, and request records stay in the ignored evidence directory.

This remains a revision by the implementing evaluator. External independent review, 28-day multi-network reliability, physical-device coverage, field INP and full citizen-service completion remain pending. Historical measurements were recomputed, not represented as newly collected measurements.
