# Ministry visual identities

Researched on 19 October 2026. Thirty identities are sourced from official ministry pages. Original artwork is preserved in meaning and colour; header emblems are cropped and raster files resized for performance. Exact source URLs, original SHA-256, derivative SHA-256 and crop rectangles are recorded in `public/ministries/sources.json`.

Four portfolios remain explicitly pending. Industry was unavailable; the internal-trade site references missing images (404). No distinct current identity was verified for mines or the state-services inspectorate. Related agencies, the startup platform mark, and historical combined-ministry logos were not substituted.

These assets identify institutions, imply no endorsement, and do not alter candidate-host validation or research scores. Each profile links to its identity source.
